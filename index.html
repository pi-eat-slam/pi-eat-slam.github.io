Physics-Informed Edge-Aware Thermal Gaussian Splatting SLAM

Overview

SLAM that runs on heat edges, not on texture

Thermal cameras see in the dark and through illumination changes, but their images are low-contrast, texture-sparse and noisy. SLAM that relies on photometric alignment or point features breaks down on them. PI-EAT-SLAM takes thermal + depth input and tracks the camera with robust geometric edges. A Stefan-Boltzmann rescaling step restores contrast using the physics of thermal radiation. A multi-stage Gradient-aware Edge-KLT (GE-KLT) tracker keeps only reliable edge correspondences, and an edge-aware smoothness loss keeps the 3D Gaussian map sharp at boundaries and smooth elsewhere.

The problem

Thermal images break conventional SLAM

Conventional SLAM relies on point feature matching or photometric loss. Both degrade in low-contrast, feature-sparse thermal images. Existing thermal SLAM often adds an IMU or LiDAR, which brings time synchronization, calibration and payload costs.

Method

Physics in, edges out: three components

1

Stefan-Boltzmann law-based rescalingSec. IV-A · preprocessing

Thermal sensors record 14/16-bit data that must be squeezed into 8 bits for tracking. Linear min-max scaling of raw intensities loses thermal detail. We first convert each raw value to absolute temperature \(T\) using the camera manufacturer's formula, then to radiance with the Stefan-Boltzmann law, and normalize the radiance to 8 bits. The constants \(\epsilon\) and \(\sigma\) cancel in the normalization, so exact emissivity is not needed. CLAHE and a bilateral filter then boost local contrast and attenuate noise.

radiance (Eq. 4)\(L(x,y)=\epsilon\,\sigma\,T(x,y)^4\)
8-bit rescaling (Eq. 5)\(I_{\text{scaled}}(x,y)=\dfrac{L(x,y)-L_{\min}}{L_{\max}-L_{\min}}\times 255\)

3

Edge-aware smoothness loss for mappingSec. IV-E · mapping

Standard smoothness losses also smooth away important geometric boundaries. Ours weights the smoothness term by \(1-w_{\text{edge}}\): it smooths non-edge regions to suppress thermal radiance artifacts and keeps sharp boundaries in the rendered images.

edge-aware smoothness (Eq. 16)\(L_{\text{e-smooth}}=\dfrac{1}{4M}\sum_{i,j}\big(|R_{i\pm1,j}-R_{i,j}|+|R_{i,j\pm1}-R_{i,j}|\big)\cdot\big(1-w_{\text{edge}}(i,j)\big)\)
edge weight (Eq. 17)\(w_{\text{edge}}(i,j)=\sigma\big(\alpha(|\nabla R_{i,j}|-\beta)\big)\)

Physics-grounded renderingSec. IV-D · rendering

To improve rendering fidelity without extra optimization overhead, we plug in the pre-trained Atmospheric Transmission Field (ATF), which suppresses floating artifacts, and the Thermal Conduction Module (TCM), which sharpens blurred boundaries, both from Thermal3D-GS.

ATF (Eq. 14)\(SH=SH_0\,e^{(\mu_{abs}+\mu_{sca})d}\)
TCM (Eq. 15)\(\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial t}=\alpha\Big(\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}+\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}\Big)\)
Evaluation

Superior tracking, competitive rendering
